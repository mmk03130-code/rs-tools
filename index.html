RS tools – 50+ Free Client-Side Web Utilities

A high-performance, open-source suite of 50+ private browser-based utilities engineered for software engineers, digital creators, students, and professionals. All data processing occurs 100% on your local machine using modern WebAssembly, Canvas 2D, and Web Crypto APIs. Your images, PDFs, code files, and sensitive documents never touch a remote cloud server.

15 Client-Side Image Tools

High-fidelity raster and vector manipulation tools operating entirely within HTML5 Canvas memory buffers without uploading files to remote servers:

  • Image Compressor

    Compress PNG, JPG, and WebP images by up to 85% with adjustable quality targets and zero server latency.

  • Image Format Converter

    Convert seamlessly between WebP, AVIF, PNG, JPG, and BMP formats with instant client-side canvas rendering.

  • Background Remover

    Chroma-key background removal with Redmean color distance analysis, dynamic variance loops, and 2D spatial Gaussian edge feathering.

  • Image Resizer & Crop

    Scale pixel dimensions with strict aspect ratio locking and custom preset crops (1:1, 16:9, 4:3).

  • Watermark & EXIF Scrubber

    Protect digital assets with text watermarks and strip private metadata tags including GPS coordinates and camera serial numbers.

  • QR Code & Favicon Generator

    Generate crisp vector QR codes and export complete multi-resolution favicon asset bundles (16x16, 32x32, 180x180, 512x512).

15 Browser-Based PDF Utilities

Manipulate portable document formats entirely in your browser using client-side WebAssembly and pure binary stream decoders:

  • Merge & Split PDF

    Combine multi-page documents or split specific page ranges with instant client-side binary stream manipulation.

  • PDF to Images (Rasterizer)

    Render vector PDF pages to 300 DPI high-definition PNG/JPG snapshots with interactive previews and bulk downloads.

  • Compress & Optimize PDF

    Shrink document filesize by compressing embedded cross-reference tables and stripping duplicate font objects.

  • Extract Text from PDF

    Asynchronous data-stream character traversal decoding embedded glyph vectors into clean, structured paragraphs.

  • Protect & Unlock PDF

    Apply cryptographic security wrappers to PDF streams or remove owner protection restrictions right in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Are my files uploaded to your servers?

No. RS Tools executes 100% of calculations in your local browser sandbox using WebAssembly, Canvas 2D, and Web Crypto. Documents never leave your device.

Is RS Tools free to use?

Yes, all 50+ utilities are completely free and open-source under the MIT license, with no registration or paywalls.

Does the ATS Resume Builder guarantee parser compatibility?

Yes. Our resume engine generates clean single-stream vector PDFs without complex multi-column floating blocks, ensuring 100% compliance with Taleo, Workday, Greenhouse, and Lever ATS systems.
